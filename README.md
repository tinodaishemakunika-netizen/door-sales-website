# MakTech Doors - Sales Website

Welcome to the official sales website for **MakTech Doors**. This is a professional platform to showcase our premium door products and track sales performance.

## About MakTech Doors

MakTech Doors is a leading premium door supplier offering a wide variety of high-quality door types for residential, commercial, and industrial applications.

**Our Promise:** Security. Style. Durability.

## Features

✅ **Professional Product Catalog** - Browse our complete selection of door types with detailed descriptions and pricing

📊 **Sales Tracker** - Real-time sales statistics and revenue tracking for all door products

💰 **Pricing Information** - Transparent pricing for all door types and options

📱 **Responsive Design** - Access from any device (desktop, tablet, mobile)

🎨 **Custom Branding** - Upload your own logo to personalize the website

- Product status indicators

### Product Showcase
Organized product cards with:

Complete contact form and business information:
- Phone and email

- Business hours
- Contact form for inquiries

## Getting Started

1. Clone this repository
2. Open `index.html` in your browser
3. Upload your MakTech Doors logo
4. Update sales data as needed

## Deploying with GitHub Pages

1. Go to repository Settings
2. Scroll to "GitHub Pages"
3. Select "main" branch as source
4. Your site will be live at: `https://tinodaishemakunika-netizen.github.io/door-sales-website/`

## Customization

You can easily customize:
- **Colors** - Edit color codes in `css/style.css` (currently: Navy #1a2a3a and Red #ff4444)
- **Products** - Add or remove product cards in `index.html`
- **Sales Data** - Update the sales table with current information
- **Contact Info** - Update phone, email, and address in the contact section
